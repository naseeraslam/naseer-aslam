---
title: "B2B Ordering Module"
description: "A Magento 2 module built for high-traffic storefronts that lets partner companies self-register, receive assigned credit limits, and purchase products in bulk directly through the storefront."
tags: ["Magento 2", "PHP", "GraphQL", "B2B Commerce"]
---

Built for high-traffic Magento storefronts, this module streamlines B2B purchasing by allowing partner businesses to register themselves, get assigned a credit limit by admins, and place bulk orders without leaving the standard storefront experience — removing the need for manual account setup and quote-based ordering.
